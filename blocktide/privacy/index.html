Privacy Policy for Blocktide

Effective date: 1 October 2026 · Developer: Tulvern · Contact: tulvern.dev@gmail.com

Blocktide ("the game") is a single-player puzzle game for Android published by Tulvern. This policy explains what information the game handles.

Information the game stores on your device

The game saves your best scores, your Daily Challenge streak, and your settings (sound, music, vibration, whether the tutorial was shown). This data stays on your device in the game's private storage. It is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Information we collect

We, Tulvern, do not collect, store, or receive any personal information from players. The game has no account system, no chat, and no analytics of our own. It works offline.

Advertising

The game shows ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (from which an approximate location may be derived), device type and operating system, and information about the ads shown and interactions with them. This processing is governed by Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy and Google's advertising terms at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and certain US states), the game shows a consent dialog before any ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time from the game's Settings screen under "Privacy options". You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings under Privacy > Ads, and opt out of ad personalisation there.

Children

Blocktide is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Permissions

  • Vibrate: optional haptic feedback, off by default.
  • Internet and Network state: used only by the advertising SDK to load ads.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above.
