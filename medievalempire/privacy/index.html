Privacy Policy for Medieval Empire

Effective date: 24 November 2026 · Developer: Tulvern · Contact: tulvern.dev@gmail.com

Medieval Empire: Idle Tycoon ("the game") is a single-player idle game for Android published by Tulvern. This policy explains what information the game handles.

Information the game stores on your device

The game saves your progress (chapters, buildings, coins, gems, deeds), a record of what you own from the shop, and your settings (music, sound, vibration). This data stays on your device in the game's private storage. It is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Information we collect

We, Tulvern, do not collect, store, or receive any personal information from players. The game has no account system, no chat, and no analytics of our own. It can be played offline.

Advertising

The game shows rewarded video ads, and only when you choose to watch one for a reward. The ads are served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (from which an approximate location may be derived), device type and operating system, and information about the ads shown and interactions with them. This processing is governed by Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy and Google's advertising terms at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and certain US states), the game shows a consent dialog before any ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time from the game's Settings screen under "Privacy options". You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings under Privacy > Ads, and opt out of ad personalisation there.

Purchases

The game offers optional purchases. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play: we never see or store your card details, name, or address. Google Play tells the game only that a purchase was made, so the game can give you the item and give it back after a reinstall. Purchases are governed by Google's privacy policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.

Children

Medieval Empire is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Permissions

  • Vibrate: short haptic feedback; it can be switched off in Settings.
  • Internet and Network state: used by the advertising SDK to load ads and by Google Play to handle purchases.
  • Advertising ID: used by the advertising SDK, as described above.
  • Google Play billing: lets Google Play handle purchases.

Deleting your data

Everything the game stores is on your device: uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in your Android settings, removes it. We hold nothing about you to delete. For data held by Google (advertising and purchases), use the controls in your Google account.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above.
